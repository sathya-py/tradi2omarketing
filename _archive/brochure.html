Risk Management

Survival first, profits second. These rules are non-negotiable in TRADI2O.

  • Risk ≤ 1–2% per trade: No single trade should break you.
  • Always hedge leveraged positions: Futures/options need protection.
  • Liquidity check before entry: Can you exit when needed?
  • Stop loss = survival: Not optional. Live to trade another day.

Institutional Execution Algorithms

Execute like FIIs and DIIs. Smart order routing minimizes market impact and optimizes fill quality for orders above ₹50,000.

TWAP Time-Weighted

Split large orders across time windows. Perfect for accumulating positions without price spikes.

VWAP Volume-Weighted

Match volume profiles with 10-30% participation. Benchmark execution within 10bps 95% of the time.

Iceberg Hidden

Only 5% of order visible at any time. Intelligent refresh prevents detection by algos.

Adaptive Smart

Auto-switches between strategies based on market conditions. >90% optimal selection rate.
